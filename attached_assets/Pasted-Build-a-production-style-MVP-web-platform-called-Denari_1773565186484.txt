Build a production-style MVP web platform called "Denarixx OneEarth".

Denarixx OneEarth is a unified AI infrastructure platform with 3 integrated modules:

1. Denarixx Energy
- Monitors solar generation, battery storage, community load, and energy alerts
- Shows site cards for different communities or facilities
- Includes charts for solar output, battery %, and energy demand
- Includes alerts such as low battery, inverter fault, overload warning

2. Denarixx LifeMesh
- Human safety and emergency response module
- Allows users to trigger SOS alerts
- Shows family members / protected persons
- Shows emergency incidents, safety status, last known location, and contact chain
- Includes cards for child safety, elderly monitoring, and emergency escalation

3. Denarixx EarthShield
- Disaster intelligence and protection module
- Shows flood risk, wildfire risk, storm risk, and infrastructure warning status
- Includes a map view with risk zones
- Includes regional alert cards and preparedness metrics
